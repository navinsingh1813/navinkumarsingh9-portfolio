About Me

Hi, I’m Navin Kumar Singh, a Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate with a strong interest in data analytics and dashboard development. I enjoy working with data, discovering meaningful patterns, and turning complex information into simple insights that support better business decisions.

I primarily work with Power BI, SQL Server, and Excel, with hands-on experience in ETL processes, data cleaning, data modeling, and interactive dashboard development. I also use Power BI Service for report publishing, collaboration, and data security.

In real-world analytics projects, I’ve strengthened both my technical and problem-solving skills:

  • Built a Credit Card Analytics Dashboard by analyzing 667K+ transactions and generating insights from $57M in revenue through KPI reporting and trend analysis.


What I enjoy most about data analytics is transforming raw data into meaningful stories and actionable insights. I’m continuously learning, improving my skills, and exploring better ways to build impactful data-driven solutions.
